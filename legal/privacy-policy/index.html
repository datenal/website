Introduction

Datenal Technologies (Pty) Ltd is committed to protecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal information. We understand the importance of privacy in the digital age and take our responsibility to protect your data seriously.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, use our services, or interact with us. It is designed to comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and other applicable South African privacy laws.

By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Information – name, surname, company name, phone number, email address, job title.
  • Business Information – company details, project requirements, service requests, industry type.
  • Technical Information – IP address, browser type, operating system, device information, pages visited, referral URLs, cookies, and website usage analytics.
  • Communication Information – emails, contact form submissions, enquiries, support requests, and any other correspondence.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Provide and manage our services
  • Respond to enquiries and prepare quotations
  • Manage projects and provide customer support
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Send important service notifications and updates
  • Meet legal obligations and prevent fraud
  • Enhance security and protect our systems

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us improve your browsing experience. We use cookies for:

  • Essential website functionality
  • Remembering preferences and settings
  • Analytics to understand how visitors use our site
  • Performance improvements

You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. However, this may affect some features of our website.

Third-Party Services

We may engage trusted third-party providers to help us deliver our services, including:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Analytics platforms (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Payment processors
  • Cloud service providers
  • Email and communication platforms
  • Software vendors

These providers are contractually obligated to process your information only as necessary to deliver their services and in compliance with applicable privacy laws.

Information Sharing

Datenal Technologies does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To deliver services you have requested (e.g., with subcontractors)
  • With your explicit consent
  • To protect our legal rights or prevent fraud

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Secure systems and access controls
  • Encryption where appropriate
  • Password protection and authentication
  • Regular security monitoring and updates

Please note that no data transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide and manage our services
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
  • Maintain business records

When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

Your Rights

Under POPIA and other applicable laws, you have the following rights:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information (where legally applicable)
  • Withdraw consent for processing (where consent is required)
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the "Contact Us" section below.

External Links

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
