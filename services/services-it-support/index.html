Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What businesses do you support?
We support small to medium-sized businesses, startups, and organisations across various industries including retail, corporate, education, healthcare, and more.
Do you offer remote IT support?
Yes, we provide fast remote support for many technical issues, allowing us to resolve problems without needing an on-site visit.
Do you provide on-site support?
Absolutely. When a problem requires physical attention, we send a technician to your premises to resolve the issue professionally.
Can you maintain our computers?
Yes, we offer preventative maintenance, updates, and regular health checks to keep your systems running smoothly and reduce future problems.
Can you help with slow computers?
We diagnose and resolve performance issues, from hardware upgrades to software optimisation, to restore speed and reliability.
Do you remove viruses and malware?
Yes, we remove viruses, spyware, and other malware, and we also advise on best practices to help protect your systems going forward.
Do you install software?
We install, configure, and update business software and applications to ensure they work correctly with your existing systems.
Can you support small businesses?
Yes, we specialise in providing affordable, practical IT support tailored to the needs and budgets of small businesses.
How quickly can you respond?
We prioritise urgent issues and aim to respond as quickly as possible. Remote support can often begin within hours, and on-site visits are scheduled at the earliest convenience.
