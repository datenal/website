Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What hosting package is right for my business?
The right package depends on your website size, traffic, and specific needs. Our team can assess your requirements and recommend the best solution. Contact us for a free consultation.
Can you transfer my existing website?
Yes. We handle the entire migration process, including planning, secure data transfer, testing, and go-live support to minimise downtime.
Do you provide business email hosting?
Yes. We offer professional email hosting with your domain, spam protection, and reliable uptime to strengthen your business communication.
Are SSL certificates included?
Yes. All our hosting packages include free SSL certificates to secure your website and protect customer data.
Can I upgrade my hosting package later?
Absolutely. Our hosting solutions are designed to scale with your business. You can upgrade at any time as your needs grow.
How secure are your hosting solutions?
We implement security best practices including firewalls, malware monitoring, daily backups, and secure infrastructure to protect your data.
Do you provide technical support?
Yes. Our team provides responsive technical support to help with any hosting-related questions or issues you may encounter.
How often are backups performed?
Backup frequency depends on your package. Starter plans include weekly backups, Business plans include daily backups, and Enterprise plans include hourly backups.
