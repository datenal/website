Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website?
Timelines vary based on complexity. A typical business website takes 4-8 weeks, but we work with you to establish a clear schedule from the start.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Absolutely. We specialise in modernising outdated websites with improved design, functionality, and user experience.
Will my website work on mobile devices?
Yes. Every website we build is fully responsive and tested to perform flawlessly across desktops, tablets, and smartphones.
Can I update my website myself?
Yes. We can build websites with easy-to-use content management systems so you can make simple updates yourself.
Do you provide website hosting assistance?
We provide guidance on hosting and domain registration, and can assist with deployment to ensure a smooth launch.
Will my website be optimised for search engines?
Yes. We build websites with strong SEO foundations including clean code, fast loading, and proper metadata to support visibility over time.
Can you build an online store?
Yes, we develop secure e-commerce websites that allow customers to browse products and complete purchases with confidence.
Do you provide ongoing website maintenance?
Yes, we offer maintenance packages that include updates, backups, security checks, and performance monitoring.
How much does a professional website cost?
Costs depend on the complexity and features required. We provide clear quotes based on your specific needs.
Can you help if I already have a domain name?
Yes, we can work with your existing domain and hosting setup, or help you migrate to a better solution.
